Most portfolios are a grid of screenshots and a list of logos. You're asked to take the work on trust. I wanted the opposite: every project on this site is written up as an **experiment**, with a hypothesis, a method, a result and the things I'd change, and each one has something you can poke at.

## What's on the bench

There are eight experiments, and they don't have much in common on the surface:

- **Midweek Merchant** forecasts Fantasy Premier League points and picked a team that beat FPL's own prediction in 28 of 37 gameweeks, without peeking.
- **Oraimo Lab** reverse-engineers a smartwatch's Bluetooth protocol and refuses to guess a single byte.
- **WC26** predicted the 2026 World Cup before it started, and the write-up shows exactly how wrong it was.
- The rest cover health, fashion, chaos engineering, Linux packaging and learning.

What they share is a habit: decide what "working" means before you start, then measure it honestly. That includes publishing the misses.

## How these notes will work

These lab notes are where the longer stories go: post-mortems, things that took a week to figure out, and essays that don't fit in a case study. New posts show up in the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) the day they're published.
