<Section n="01" title="The problem">

Students get a semester's worth of slides and no map through them. Revision turns into rereading, which feels productive and isn't. Tutilo starts from the material you already have and builds the structure you're missing.

</Section>

<Section n="02" title="What it does">

- **Blueprint:** upload notes or slides and the AI maps the key concepts into a visual learning path, so you know where to start.
- **Concept map:** the course as a connected graph, so relationships between topics are visible and the next thing to study is obvious.
- **Practice:** adaptive quizzes, flashcards for active recall, and a tutor chat for instant help.
- **Formats:** text, audio and video explainers, plus a Feynman mode where you teach the topic back.

Pricing is in shillings and built for students: a free tier with a cheaper model and five uploads, then Plus and Pro for URL sources, smarter maps, video explanations and unlimited notebooks.

<Aside>Pricing in shillings, not dollars, is a product decision as much as a currency one.</Aside>
</Section>

</Section>
